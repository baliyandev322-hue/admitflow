# AdmitFlow AI — Institute Owner & Administrator Guide

This guide is designed for coaching institute owners, directors, and operational administrators managing multi-branch centers, subscription quotas, and AI tool policies.

---

## 1. Multi-Branch Operations (`/branches`)

* **Center Performance Head-to-Head:**
  * Compare lead volumes, conversion percentages, and confirmed admissions across branches (e.g. *Kota*, *Jaipur*, *Delhi*).
* **Branch-Level Access:**
  * Branch managers can only inspect and manage leads assigned to their physical center.
  * Institute owners enjoy unified multi-campus visibility.

---

## 2. Model Context Protocol (MCP) & Tool Permissions (`/integrations`)

* **Atomic Tool Management:**
  * View all 33 registered MCP tools categorized by CRM, Counselling, RAG Knowledge, Analytics, Calendar, WhatsApp, and Documents.
  * Toggle tools ON or OFF instantly based on institute policies.
* **Audit Trail Inspector:**
  * Monitor real-time tool execution logs, including actor attribution, payload inputs, and millisecond latency.

---

## 3. Subscription & Quota Management (`/billing`)

* **Live Quota Meters:**
  * Track lead ingestion quota (e.g. 10,000 leads/mo on Pro Plan).
  * Track RAG vector query quota (e.g. 5,000 queries/mo).
* **Plan Upgrades via Razorpay:**
  * Upgrade subscription tiers seamlessly with verified server-side HMAC signatures.

---

## 4. Pilot Feedback & Bug Reporting Process

* **Direct Counselor Feedback:**
  * In the bottom navigation, click **Feedback** or report issues directly to the DevOps team at `support@admitflow.ai`.
* **Urgent Production Escalations:**
  * Critical security or billing inquiries are prioritized with 1-hour response SLA.
