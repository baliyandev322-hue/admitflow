# AdmitFlow AI — Admission Counselor Training Checklist
**Target Team**: Admission Counselors & Tele-calling Staff (Sunita Sharma & Vikram Rathore)  
**Objective**: Master the CRM Kanban, Student Timeline, AI Recommendations & Conflict-Free Slot Booking in 30 minutes.

---

## 1. CRM Kanban Basics
- [ ] **Logging In**: Access `https://app.admitflow.ai/login` using your official credentials (`sunita.sharma@prernacareer.edu.in`).
- [ ] **Pipeline Columns**:
  - `NEW`: Fresh leads captured by the AI chat widget or walk-ins.
  - `CONTACTED`: First tele-call or WhatsApp intro sent.
  - `INTERESTED`: Student/parent confirmed course interest & eligibility.
  - `COUNSELLING_SCHEDULED`: Campus visit or video call slot booked.
  - `TRIAL_CLASS`: Student registered for Kota / Jaipur demo lecture.
  - `ADMISSION_CONFIRMED`: Fee installment deposited.
  - `LOST`: Student joined elsewhere or dropped plan.
- [ ] **Drag & Drop**: Practice moving a lead between stages. Confirm activity history auto-records status change.

---

## 2. Reading the Student Activity Timeline
- [ ] Open a student card to see their complete history:
  - Exact questions asked to the AI assistant (e.g., fee queries, hostel doubts).
  - Calculated AI Lead Score (e.g., 60-95 based on buying signals).
  - Next Best Action suggestion calculated by AdmitFlow AI.
- [ ] Verify that DPDP consent timestamp is recorded before initiating any outbound WhatsApp message.

---

## 3. Booking Counseling Slots (No Double Booking)
- [ ] Click "Schedule Counseling" on a lead.
- [ ] Select branch (`Indraprastha Main Campus Kota` or `Jaipur Center`).
- [ ] Select counselor and preferred date/time slot (e.g., 2026-04-10 10:00 AM).
- [ ] Observe conflict detection: If the counselor already has an appointment at that time, AdmitFlow will block the booking with an instant conflict warning.

---

## 4. One-Click WhatsApp Follow-ups
- [ ] Review pending follow-up recommendations generated by the AI agent.
- [ ] Click "Approve & Send" to dispatch the pre-approved WhatsApp follow-up template.
- [ ] If a student replies `STOP`, verify that their lead status displays `WHATSAPP OPTED OUT` and outbound templates are locked.

---

## 5. Daily Discipline
- [ ] Log in at 09:30 AM every morning.
- [ ] Review all leads in `NEW` within 15 minutes.
- [ ] Complete scheduled follow-ups before 01:00 PM.
- [ ] Update lead status immediately after each parent phone call or campus walk-in.
