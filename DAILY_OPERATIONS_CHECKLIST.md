# AdmitFlow AI — Daily Pilot Operations Checklist
**Role**: Center Manager & Front Desk Admission Team  
**Cadence**: Daily (Morning, Afternoon, Evening)  

---

## Morning Routine (09:00 AM – 10:00 AM)
- [ ] **Overnight Lead Sweep**:
  - Filter CRM for leads created between 08:00 PM and 09:00 AM via website widget.
  - Review AI Lead Scores (>75 indicates high-intent students asking about admissions/scholarships).
  - Assign unallocated leads to available branch counselors.
- [ ] **Today's Appointments Review**:
  - Open `/appointments` dashboard.
  - Review scheduled campus visits and counseling calls for the day.
  - Send morning appointment reminder via approved template.

---

## Afternoon Routine (01:30 PM – 03:00 PM)
- [ ] **Follow-up Approval Queue**:
  - Open `/followups` tab.
  - Review AI-suggested follow-ups (e.g., students who asked for fee structure 48 hours ago).
  - Bulk approve or customize notes before sending.
- [ ] **Trial Class & Walk-In Attendance**:
  - Mark attendance for students attending Kota/Jaipur demo classes.
  - Move attended students from `TRIAL_CLASS` to `INTERESTED` or `ADMISSION_CONFIRMED`.

---

## Evening Routine (06:30 PM – 07:30 PM)
- [ ] **Pipeline Cleanup**:
  - Verify no leads remain in `NEW` untouched for >4 hours.
  - Ensure all calls made during the day have an updated status and next follow-up date.
- [ ] **Daily Admission Tally**:
  - Record tokens or fees received at the accounts desk.
  - Move enrolled students to `ADMISSION_CONFIRMED`.
- [ ] **End-of-Day Sync**:
  - Center Manager reviews Daily Lead Conversion metric on the Analytics tab.
